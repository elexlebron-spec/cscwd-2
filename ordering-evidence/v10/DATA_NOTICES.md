municipal examples in this package. They do not apply a new blanket licence to
third-party data. Raw logs, full normalized tables, real case IDs, full case

## UCI CC BY 4.0 actions

The publisher page expressly assigns CC BY 4.0. The full licence was read.
Sections 2-4 permit reuse/adaptation subject to attribution and applicable
conditions. Creator/source/licence links and the disclaimer reference are
retained here. Changes: read CSV records, select the declared fields, replace
resource labels with log-specific aliases, group timestamps/counters, calculate
adjacency and certified summaries. No record was dropped or deduplicated in
the observed population. Five counter-conflict cases use the timestamp-only
fallback. No endorsement by UCI, ServiceNow, 4TU or the data creators is implied.
The licence does not grant unrelated privacy, trademark or patent rights, and
the source is supplied without warranties (CC BY 4.0, Sections 2(b), 5-6).

## File identity and privacy boundary

M1, M2 and M3 decompressed XES match their official file sizes/MD5, while the
BPI2013 compressed file matches directly. The BPI2012 mirrored gzip differs
from the official compressed checksum; decompressed official equivalence has
not been checked. Its historical background results remain restricted to the
fixed mirror input. All three **core** populations (M1, M3, IT) have publisher
checksum or direct official-download provenance. Raw SHA256 and full evidence
are recorded locally and in the allowlisted aggregate artifact.
